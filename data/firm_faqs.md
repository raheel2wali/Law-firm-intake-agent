# Firm knowledge base

This is what LexIntake is allowed to know. If a client asks something
that isn't in these docs, the agent says so instead of guessing.

> Sample data for the build-in-public series. A real firm would replace

## Practice areas

- Personal injury (car accidents, slip and fall, workplace injuries)
- Family law (divorce, child custody, child support)
- Estate planning (wills, trusts, probate)
- We do NOT handle criminal defense or immigration cases.

## Fees

- Initial consultations are free for the first 30 minutes.
- Personal injury cases are handled on contingency: no fee unless we win.
  The standard contingency rate is 33% of the recovery (40% if the case
  goes to trial).
- Family law and estate planning are billed hourly at $250-$350 per hour,
  depending on the attorney. A retainer is required before work begins.
- We never quote exact fees for a specific case without an attorney
  reviewing the facts first.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long will my case take?**
It depends on the facts. Personal injury cases typically settle in 6-12
months; cases that go to trial take longer. We can't promise timelines.

**Do I have to come to the office?**
No. Consultations can be by phone, video, or in person, whichever you prefer.

**What should I bring to my consultation?**
Any documents related to your case: contracts, police reports, medical
records, photos, and a timeline of what happened.

**Will my information stay confidential?**
Yes. Everything you share during intake is treated as confidential, even
if you don't end up hiring us.

## Office

- Open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM Central.
- After-hours calls get a text-back within 15 minutes.
- Se habla español. Multi-language support available on request.
- Parking is free in the lot behind the building.

## What we can't do

- We can't give legal advice over the phone or chat. Only a licensed
  attorney can do that, after reviewing your case.
- We can't tell you whether you have a case. That's an attorney's call.
- If your matter is urgent (arrest, court date within 48 hours), we
  flag it for immediate attorney review instead of normal intake.
